# Share the project as a maker story

Start with a moment someone recognises: arriving at a desk, a long sequence of meetings or wanting a short break. Then show the complete response: a sentence, a visible state change and a physical ticket.

## Useful shots

1. The assembled enclosure, phone in the right holder, plant above and paper below.
2. A real sensor change: shade the light sensor or move a flat ultrasonic target.
3. The Indicator's calibrated percentage beside YOYO's reported percentage.
4. The screen preview, one confirmation, the corresponding paper and a clean cut.
5. Ordinary reconnection with the existing bond and visible request feedback.

Show the actual phone, controller and printer routes. Do not imply that the Indicator is the phone's Bluetooth relay or that a browser button remotely controls the hardware.

## Label the medium

The assembly hero is a CAD visualization with official HONOR phone-image textures. It is not a photograph of a fully enclosed assembly. UI captures, rendered receipt layouts and physical bench photos should each be labelled accurately. Credit official product imagery and the Printr inspiration where used.

The current public material does not include a project video. Do not imply video footage exists because an official HONOR demonstration supplied a product-image reference. English project text is available in the repository under `docs/sharing/`; the hardware and test statements should stay aligned with [roadmap and evidence](roadmap-evidence.md).

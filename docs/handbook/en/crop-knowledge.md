# Crop knowledge and contextual guidance

The crop-knowledge area is a separate, source-oriented prototype. It should not be confused with the already tested sensor/display/printing loop.

The useful question is not simply “what percentage should I water at?” A plant's species, substrate, pot, root maturity, climate and placement all affect what a reading means. Soil ADC alone cannot determine a universal safe schedule, flowering date or fertilizer dose.

## How guidance should be represented

Each recommendation needs a source, crop identity, applicable stage, environmental assumptions and a description of uncertainty. Prefer primary agricultural or research sources over unsourced generated advice. Distinguish an observed event from a predicted date, and make the sensor evidence visible next to any recommendation.

The repository's [crop-knowledge integration](../../../integrations/crop-knowledge/README.md) documents the current data/prototype scope and original references. It is not an online agronomy service, a guaranteed growth predictor or a commissioned agricultural control system.

Until a rule has been matched to the actual plant and installation, use RoboPlanter for monitoring and deliberate local care. A relative calibrated soil percentage and an entertaining plant character are useful, but neither is a universal irrigation policy.
